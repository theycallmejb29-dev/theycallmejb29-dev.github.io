---
title: Main Aur Meri Parchhai
date: 2026-09-09
draft: false
---
Main aur meri parchhai, do saathi anjaane,
Ek daudti dhoop mein, ek chupti viraane.
Suraj ki pehli kiran se gehra rishta uska,
Tabhi har mod par, khud ko mazboot paaya.

Jab main chala aage, woh saath-saath chalti,
Roshni mein sang, andhere mein machalti.
Kabhi peeche chhoothti, kabhi paas aa jaati,
Mere saaye ki tarah, mere dil ko sehlaati.

Kabhi lambi, kabhi chhoti, har roop dikhati,
Jeevan ke har rang mein, apni chhavi sajati.
Mujhe dekhti, par sawaal nahi karti,
Mere har raaz ko bin kahe hi padhti.

Par kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon, kya ye sach mein sacchi hai?
Ya meri kalpnaon ki mahaz ek parchhai hai?
Kya main uski tasveer hoon, ya woh meri hai?
Sawaalon ki ye guththi har pal gehri hai.

Phir samajh aaya, ye jeevan ka aaina hai,
Main hoon iska aadhar, aur ye meri chhaya hai.
Saath jeete hain, saath marte hain,
Main aur meri parchhai, hamesha jude rehte hain.
