---
title: Kaash Aur Khair
date: 2026-09-30
draft: false
---
Kaash tum samajh paate jo main keh nahi paata,
Khair tumhara naam aate hi dil muskura jaata.

Kaash kabhi tumhari baaton mein mera bhi zikr ho
Khair tumse baat ho jaaye, bas itna hi fikr ho

Tum mere paas hoke bhi door ho,
Phir bhi na jaane kyun dil ko manzoor ho.
Na koi shikayat hai, na koi sawaal,
Bas tum achhe lagte ho, shayad yahi hai kamaal.

Kaash kabhi bheed mein tum mujhe bhi dhoondo,
Khair main toh har jagah tumhe hi chun loon.

Kaash kabhi meri kami tumhe bhi thodi si lage,
Khair tumhari ek muskaan se hi din achha lage.

Kaash tumhari nazar kabhi mujh par ruk jaaye,
Khair meri nazar toh tum tak aakar khud hi tham jaaye.

Kaash tumhe bhi meri aadat si ho jaaye,
Khair tumhari aadat bhi mujhe buri kahan lagti hai.

Na tumhe paane ki zid hai,
Na kuch jaldi se keh dene ka mann,
Bas tum aas paas raho,
Aur thoda sa achha lage har din.

Kaash kabhi ye doori thodi kam ho jaaye,
Khair jitni hai, utni bhi apni si lag jaaye.
Kaash kabhi tum meri kahaani ka hissa ban jao,
Khair abhi ke liye khayaalon mein hi sahi, bas nazar aao.

Aakhir mein bas itna sa hi khayaal hai,
Kaash tum mil jao toh kamaal hai,
Khair na bhi mile toh kya malaal hai,
Tumhe chahna hi apne aap mein
Mere dil ka sabse khoobsurat sawaal hai.

Shayad mohabbat ka matlab sirf paana nahi hota,
Kabhi kisi ko dekhkar yun muskura dena bhi hota hai.
Kaash ek din tumhe bhi ye baat samajh aaye,
Khair tab tak tum khush raho,
Mera dil toh waise bhi tumhe dekhkar khush ho jaaye.
