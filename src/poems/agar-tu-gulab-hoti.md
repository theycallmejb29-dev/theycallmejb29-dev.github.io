---
title: Agar Tu Gulab Hoti
date: 2024-08-07
draft: false
---
Main tod deta agar tu gulab hoti,
Par tu toh meri har dua ka jawab hoti.

Khushboo banke sirf lamhon mein na simat jaati,
Tu meri zindagi ki sabse haseen kitaab hoti.

Har panne pe tera naam likhta,
Har khwaab ko tere sang hi dekhta.

Chaand bhi sharma jaaye teri muskaan dekhkar,
Main har raat bas tera hi intezaar karta.

Na phoolon ki zarurat, na mehfilon ka shor,
Bas tera saath ho, toh dil maange na kuch aur.

Ishq agar kabhi alfaazon ka mohtaaj hota,
Har lafz ke baad sirf tera hi naam hota.
